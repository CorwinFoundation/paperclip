# BackBond Monthly Ecosystem Watchlist

**Status:** Internal operating ledger  
**Owner:** CMO  
**Cadence:** Monthly, first calendar day at 09:00 America/New_York, beginning 2026-08-01  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-01
**Issue authority:** BEAAA-18312  

## Purpose

Detect changes that affect BackBond's category position without reacting to isolated vendor marketing. This ledger informs internal decisions only. It does not authorize external claims, select or sequence a protection path, or unfreeze the `1,180+` BackBond Scanner figure.

## Monthly procedure

1. Review primary sources published since the prior entry: standards bodies, carrier and broker appetite guides, procurement requirements, audit-firm offerings, and product releases.
2. Update every named ledger line with dated evidence, source type, and confidence.
3. Evaluate the trigger table. One tooling vendor alone does not trigger a strategy change.
4. Record one disposition: `observe`, `prepare interoperation`, or `escalate for Founder decision`.
5. Link supporting evidence and the resulting Paperclip issue in the entry. External use of any competitor or carrier name requires the existing sign-off path.

## Trigger table

| Trigger | Evidence threshold | Default disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier or broker appetite-guide citation | AIUC-1 named in a published appetite guide or underwriting requirement | Escalate for Founder decision; assess framework interoperation |
| Procurement asks for AIUC-1 by name | Verifiable buyer requirement, RFP language, or design-partner request | Prepare interoperation; log buyer context and frequency |
| Additional tooling vendors | Two or more independent vendors selling AIUC-1 implementation or compliance tooling | Prepare interoperation; one tooling vendor remains `observe` |
| Additional auditors | Two or more independent audit firms publicly offering AIUC-1 audit or certification services | Prepare interoperation and assess procurement-badge gravity |
