What happens to your data?

The service stores your report email, agent name, endpoint host, test messages, replies, and report results. Report links stop being viewable after 180 days. Scan records are deleted after 180 days, or sooner on request to info@backbond.ai. Contact info@backbond.ai for privacy or deletion requests. Authentication details are sent to the scanner and excluded from the intake database; the complete credential lifecycle is under review.

Unless you opt out, BackBond keeps a copy of the scan's test messages, your agent's responses and the results, without your email address, credentials, endpoint or agent name, and uses it to improve its tests and scoring. To opt out, uncheck the product-improvement box before starting the scan, or email info@backbond.ai later and BackBond will delete the copy.
