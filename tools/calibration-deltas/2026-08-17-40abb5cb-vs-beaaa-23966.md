## How the zero was established

Two independent checks agree.

Structural. The candidate diff against `40abb5cb` touches `tests/test_p1_1_factuality_golden.py` and
`tests/test_p1_4_refusal_golden.py` and nothing else. No rubric file, no probe map, no judge prompt,
no scoring math, no scanner module. A change confined to assertion literals inside test files cannot
reach the scoring path, so the delta is zero by construction rather than by measurement.

Measured. `scripts/beaaa6190_v05_rc_n5.py` runs the golden set at N=5 and regenerates
`tools/calibration-deltas/2026-06-22-beaaa6190-v05-rc-n5.md`. It was run on a fresh detached worktree
at `40abb5cb` and on the candidate branch. The two generated files are byte-identical to each other
and to the committed file, and each run reports `Byte-identical to expected: True` with total spread 0
and max pillar spread 0.0000 for all six live agents.

## What this change does not fix

Two defects from the same BEAAA-22121 drift are left in place deliberately, each with its own ticket.

BEAAA-23968. `harness/bench/factuality_golden.py:161-162` and `harness/bench/p1_4_refusal_golden.py:189-190`
hardcode `corpus_version` and `probe_set_version` at `@0.1.0`, and `scripts/beaaa8127_ops_live_fire.py`
passes the same literals at its two call sites. Every P1.1 and P1.4 envelope produced since `1a1ce3c0`
therefore reports a corpus_version that does not identify the corpus that produced it. Four assertions
in this change stay pinned at `@0.1.0` to match current behaviour, each carrying a comment naming
BEAAA-23968; that ticket flips them to `@0.2.0` when it lands. Fixing it here would have changed
emitted envelope metadata inside a change whose whole purpose is to be score-neutral.

BEAAA-23969. `tools/wave0_case_inventory.py:30,32` still targets 30 and 20 probes, so
`tests/test_wave0_case_inventory.py::test_canonical_wave0_inventory` is red on master too. It sits
outside the required check, so it blocks nothing. Raising the targets alone does not fix it: the test
compares the committed canonical inventory against generated rows, so the inventory needs regenerating
and the 10 new rows need their oracle bindings reviewed.

## Unrelated pre-existing condition, noted not fixed

`tools/partner-kit/README.md`, `conformance_check.py`, and `generate_design_partner_quickstart_v2.py`
show as modified on a clean checkout of `40abb5cb`. They are committed with CRLF while `.gitattributes`
forces LF, so git reports them dirty immediately after checkout with no command run. This predates the
change and is excluded from the candidate diff. Worth a hygiene pass because `.gitattributes` states
the LF policy exists so integrity guards hashing raw file bytes stay stable; these three files are
partner-facing documents rather than hashed configuration, so nothing fails closed today.
