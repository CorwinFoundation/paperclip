## Why Δ=0 is structural, not luck

1. The N=5 harness (`scripts/beaaa6190_v05_rc_n5.py`) scores golden fixtures
   through `rubric.scoring.score` directly from `eval_outputs.json`. Its
   import graph does **not** include `scanners.live_probe_workers`,
   `scanners.live_probe_orchestrator`, or `scanners.replicate_median`
   (verified: none appear in `sys.modules` after importing the harness'
   dependencies). The code the harness exercises is byte-unchanged.
2. Even on the live-probe path, the golden roster is deterministic: every K
   replicate is identical, so `_median_of(replicates) == last replicate`.
   The fix and the baseline therefore agree on this input by construction —
   which is exactly why the pre-fix N=5 gate was structurally blind to F3.
3. The discriminating evidence is the AC#1 nondeterministic replay
   (`tests/test_beaaa14422_median_envelope.py::test_ac1_...`): with per-dim
   raw varying across K, the pre-fix Scorer scored `are_total=50` (last
   replicate) while the median-correct value is `56`. The fix scores `56`;
   baseline fails that assertion. See the ticket for the attached pre-fix
   failing output.
