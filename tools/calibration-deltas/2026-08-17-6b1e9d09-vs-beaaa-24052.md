## Ruling

Pin the derivation, do not bump the counts.

`tools/wave0_case_inventory.py` re-derived a frozen artifact from the live rubric tree. Every one of
the 715 rows in `artifacts/beaaa-15688/wave0-case-inventory.jsonl` asserts
`source_provenance.source_revision == 6e78dae4`, and `wave0-inventory-manifest.json` signs
`inventory_sha256` over the artifact bytes. `1a1ce3c` (BEAAA-22121) grew the P1.1 roster 30 to 34 and
P1.4 20 to 26, and the derivation followed the live files, so the tool raised and the required
`python pytest` check went red.

The ticket proposed bumping the two targets to 34 and 26 and called that "probably right". It is not.
Regenerating against master produces 725 rows, which invalidates the signed `inventory_sha256`, breaks
the `signature` block, and moves a figure QA approved at `fb58d5e` under BEAAA-15700. It would also
stamp `source_revision: 6e78dae4` on ten rows that did not exist at `6e78dae4`. The count table was
never a claim about the live rubric; it is a description of a snapshot, and the defect is that the tool
read the wrong tree.

The 23 mapping sources at `6e78dae4` are now vendored under `artifacts/beaaa-15688/source-pin` and the
derivation reads those. `materialize` reproduces the committed inventory byte for byte, which it could
not do on master at all.

## Independence from BEAAA-24003

The ticket asked whether to reconcile the targets with the P1.1/P1.4 floors under review on BEAAA-24003.
No reconciliation is needed. The inventory is a 2026-07 snapshot, not a scored surface; its manifest
records `n5_calibration_delta: not_applicable_inventory_only_no_score_path_change`. After this change it
does not read the live P1.1/P1.4 fixtures at all, so 24003 can set those floors to whatever the co-sign
chain lands on without touching this gate. The two tickets are now independent, and neither blocks the other.

## Tamper resistance

Net increase, no reduction.

- Before: nothing verified the artifact bytes against the signed `inventory_sha256`. An edited row paired
  with an edited `dedupe_sha256` passed `verify()`. `verify_signed_artifact()` closes that.
- Before: no check that a Wave 0 source was the one the inventory was derived from. `pinned_bytes()` fails
  closed on any hash mismatch, and `verify_source_pin()` walks all 24 pinned entries.
- `test_source_pin_is_byte_identical_to_source_revision` re-reads the blobs from `6e78dae4` and compares.
  It skips on a shallow clone, which is what `actions/checkout@v4` gives the `python pytest` job, so it is
  a local and full-clone guard rather than a CI one.
- What the gate no longer refuses: live rubric growth. That was never a defect the artifact could describe.
