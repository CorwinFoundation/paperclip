# Acceptance scoring rubric v1

Scorer identities must be recorded with the benchmark artifact. Design health is 40 points: hierarchy 8, BackBond recognition 8, density and scanability 8, responsive and print coherence 8, and absence of generic visual treatment 8. A blind CMO/Designer review scores the unlabeled pair and must reach 32/40 with no P0/P1 defect.

Technical audit is 20 points: keyboard traversal 4, 200% zoom without unresolved overflow 4, reduced motion 3, landmark structure 3, measurable focus visibility 3, and WCAG contrast 3. The deterministic audit computes evidence; an independent QA reviewer assigns the final score and severity disposition. Passing automated checks is not publication or claims approval.
