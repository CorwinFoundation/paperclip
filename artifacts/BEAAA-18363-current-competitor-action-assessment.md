# Current competitor action assessment

**Issue:** BEAAA-18363  
**Audience:** CEO and Founder  
**Owner:** CMO  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** Internal competitive intelligence. This record does not authorize external competitor claims, a positioning change, or a change to the three-wrapper ladder.

## Decision

**Act on execution, monitor category proof, make no strategy change.**

AIUC has the strongest direct proof against BackBond's current wedge: Intercom says AIUC-1 certification helped turn procurement friction into differentiation for a customer-support agent, and AIUC describes quarterly testing across 1,000+ scenarios. Testudo is widening insured perils for enterprise buyers. Armilla continues to validate vendor-side warranty demand. None combines BackBond's intended vendor-embedded distribution, continuously perishable public score, two-sensor underwriting loop, and per-agent, per-action, per-peril model.

That distinction is architectural until BackBond puts a design partner live. The competitive response is one accepted trust-page plus warranty deployment, not another claim.
